## Contract text (committed, `docs/bounded-runtime-contract.md`)
"The work budget": "One unit visits a record field or a frame/temporary owner, or finalizes a task. A record unit
examines at most eight fields and visits the next field that owns a reference; fields that own nothing share that unit,
so a record whose compared fields own nothing costs fewer units than it has fields. A unit that reaches the record's last
field also finalizes the record, still as one unit. The work of a unit therefore stays constant: at most eight field
examinations and one owner release."
"Scheduling and batching": "Record fields share one unit up to the eight-field examination limit above; the next field
that owns a reference, or the record's last field (with its finalization), ends that unit."
Guarantees: at most K units per service point (measured max 32 = K), constant work per unit, K per operation table
untouched, no API change, no allocation/queue/order change, no release skipped, deterministic, identical outputs.
