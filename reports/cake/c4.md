## What changed (runtime/minyar_bounded_rc.h, docs/bounded-runtime-contract.md)

- `rc_bounded_retire_small_in`: a unit takes the small aggregate it starts from, retires its owner slots and
  takes each uniquely owned small child (record ≤ `RC_SMALL_RECORD_SLOTS` = 8 slots, reference List ≤
  `RC_SMALL_LIST_SLOTS` = 8 slots) onto a fixed stack until `RC_SMALL_RECORD_UNIT_OBJECTS` = 8 aggregates are
  retired. Everything else follows the old path (leaf freed in the unit, aggregate queued). Objects finalize
  newest-first. No recursion, no allocation; work per unit is constant, so K units still bound a service point.
- Both object units and both contiguous runs enter it; the unary-pair special case is gone (the drain is
  strictly cheaper and keeps the order).
- Contract text: "A small aggregate — a record with at most eight slots, or a reference List with at most eight
  slots — is retired by one unit … up to eight aggregates per unit"; the two unary-pair bullets are removed;
  the per-operation table says a public release's K includes small-aggregate destruction.

## Guarantees checked and how

- **K bound**: `check-bounded` (system/fixed/lazy at K 1/2/8/9/32/1024) asserts at most
  `K × RC_SMALL_RECORD_UNIT_OBJECTS` objects retire per service point and pins a 100 000-record chain's remaining
  units; `check-backlog-pacing`, `check-unary-pairs`, `check-fused-unary`, `check-direct-service` (direct frame
  exit vs the scheduler at 18 budgets × backlog configurations) and `check-owner-queue-batching` pass.
- **Memory safety**: `check-bounded`, `check-memory-profiles`, `check-compact-ownership`, `check-scope-ownership`,
  `check-text-view-budget`, `check-temporary-owner-cache` (ASan/UBSan lanes included).
- **Determinism / ABI**: runtime only; no object layout or header change; outputs byte-identical on all 22
  compare programs (`same yes`).
- API unchanged: **yes**.
