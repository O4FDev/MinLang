**Finding 1 — high, blocking: parallel branch arms falsely prove a bounds-success edge.** In the pinned checker, `reachable` at lines 390–397 removes all successor entries matching a `(source,target)` pair. The slot-load proof at 705–714 removes `(gb, branch.labels[0])` and interprets loss of reachability as true-edge dominance. LLVM permits both arms of a conditional branch to target the same block. Removing that pair therefore removes the false arm too, falsely establishing that the bounds comparison succeeded.

The complete, small, well-typed [same-target getter input](evidence/local-cleanup-certifier-implementation-review/same-target-getter.ll) is retained with independently specified rejection expectation in [probe-manifest.json](evidence/local-cleanup-certifier-implementation-review/probe-manifest.json). Its decisive instructions are:

```llvm
%valid = icmp ult i64 %position, %length
br i1 %valid, label %good, label %good
```

The root constructs one List, appends one element, transfers its producer once, then calls the getter at position 1. Length is 1 and capacity is 2. The comparison is false, yet control proceeds to the raw backing load. The checker returns `conditional_component_certificate`, V=1/F=2/W=3. This is a counterexample to membership in the required guarded-read subset and to O3's bounds proof. It is not a demonstrated undercharge of the structural owner/chunk/frame count. Loading spare backing storage does not gain a logical List-bounds proof from the generic valid-success premise; that premise cannot substitute for the required dominating true edge. No generated input was executed.
