# Fixed-credit reuse: closest precedents and falsification plan

Reviewed online on 4 October 2026 after the generated 65-member retired-List workload failed to unlock reuse with an extra K-unit poll. No new algorithm or novelty claim follows from that failure. The useful research question is whether changing the placement or order of **already available** reclamation units improves reuse without worsening other admission or latency cases. The distinction between a compiler insertion, runtime service hook and actual executed unit must remain explicit.

## Operation accounting that an experiment must preserve

Current `join_by_copying` requests byte data and a Text header; both allocations service up to K queued units. The consuming fallback subsequently releases the left owner, sharing its release budget with immediate destruction. An already-unique Text with spare backing performs no allocation service; growing a unique Text has one reallocation hook. Consequently, unconditional pre-2K polling does not preserve the executed service of the already-unique/spare path. Two K polls are not one K-bounded operation.

A test-only relocation must record initial uniqueness, baseline hook sites, each requested budget, actual executed units and immediate destruction. Suppress only explicitly displaced allocation hooks through local helper parameters or separate test-only primitives. A global suppression flag adds restoration/reentrancy obligations and can accidentally affect unrelated allocation. The fallback's final consuming release remains accounted separately. No credits may silently be borrowed from a future statement or a path that would not execute. Eligibility changing after polling is a measured consequence, not evidence that the branch's original work was free.

## Minimal experiment and rejection controls

| Variant | Intended test | Falsifier or required opposite case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Baseline current scheduler | Generated65-member batch, caller Text in last slot, exact public compiler/runtime path | Establish actual ownership count and queue progress at the join; do not replace this with private isolated setup. |
| Relocate one existing fallback allocation hook | Initial nonunique consuming left; move one K service before the repeated uniqueness test | Same actual/hook credit accounting; existing negative case may still miss its owner. Preserve already-unique fast path. |
| Relocate two existing fallback allocation hooks | Same workload, suppress both displaced hooks locally on copying path | Compare total public-operation units with its explicit baseline allowance; live alias, view and borrowed-self controls cannot acquire false uniqueness. |
| Reverse retired List owner visits | Constant-index reverse traversal, unchanged number/cost of visits and task transitions | Caller Text in first slot must expose the opposite loss. Add competing old objects, frame and chunk tasks; preserve their service fairness. LIFO itself is prior art. |
| Compiler-known owner ordering | Statically order a known retirement sequence by a nearby reuse candidate, without a runtime queue scan | Unknown index/alias/escaping container must use the conservative path. Source effect order, stored List order and borrowed live values must remain intact. No extra syntax or metadata growth. |

Use widths 1,2K−1,2K,2K+1,65,1025, positions first/middle/last, K 1/8/32, both compact and deliberately oversized Text backing, eager/system/fixed/lazy controls. Test exact public schedules with live aliases, indexed Unicode, views, sparse moved locals, competing owner tasks and repeated negative decisions. Time measurements require serialized repeated trials and counter instrumentation separated from timed builds.

Admission is a first-class result. Equal total decrement units do not determine which blocks are available before each allocation, and successful reuse may retain a much larger backing than copying. Add a subsequent unrelated request sized between the two remaining-free-space states and an opposite-priority debt object whose freed buddy is needed. Report copying bytes, header/backing identity, requested/charged bytes, peak retention, exact final recovery and baseline-success/candidate-OOM asymmetry. Do not promote an optimization from reuse rate alone.

A defensible potential result is a carefully scoped counterexample family or acceptance condition: per-hook K caps and equal cumulative work alone are insufficient to preserve finite-pool admission under changed allocation/retirement order. Its general observation has antecedents in allocator- and schedule-dependent GC results above. Whether the exact compiler-generated fixed-credit workload, criterion or transformation constitutes a research contribution remains open; a paper claim needs stronger formalization, broader controls and further primary-source falsification.
