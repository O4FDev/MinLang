# Automatic memory literature audit, 4 October 2026

This pass extends the [earlier 51-source register](../novelty/sources.md), rather than replacing its findings. The practical target is unchanged source syntax, shared observable mutation, single-mutator non-atomic ownership, finite managed capacity and predictable cleanup service. The strongest current outcome is a set of falsifiable engineering hypotheses, not a claim of a new universal memory-management technique. Exact candidates and experiments are in [literature-falsification.md](literature-falsification.md); implementation comparisons are in [literature-peers.md](literature-peers.md).

## Search and evidence protocol

1. Start from the actual runtime contract and prior negative results. Separate reference-count update, owner retirement, graph traversal, allocator metadata, copying, capacity and hardware time. A paper about one of these does not prove all of them.
2. Search mechanism names and synonyms, including `deferred decrements`, `lazy reference counting`, `dead roots`, `last-use`, `space-safe borrowing`, `reuse`, `partial tracing`, `constant-time reference counting`, `region inference`, `peak heap`, `cycle collection`, `event-driven scheduling` and `GC work packets`. Add years 2025 and 2026 and inspect publication or revision dates, not crawler dates.
3. Prefer author manuscripts, publisher full texts, official institutional repositories and official runtime source. Search-engine excerpts identify leads; secondary summaries do not support technical findings. Use official language documentation for documented behavior and source for implementation observations.
4. Chain selected references backwards from CTRC, frame-limited reuse, concurrent immediate RC, RTRC and Work Packets. Follow recent author pages, publication records and artifacts forwards. This is selected citation chaining; no citation graph or venue census was fully traversed.
5. Record reading depth and missing access. **F** means full text accessible and selected relevant sections inspected, not every proof checked. **A** means primary abstract or institutional/author record only. **D** means official documentation/source. **T** means author slides. **S** means indexed primary material screened but opening failed.
6. Before a novelty claim, extract the exact algorithm and theorem of the closest work and compare assumptions, costs and observability. Search absence is insufficient. For performance, pin tools and use equal semantics and workloads before interpreting ratios.

The [search log](literature-search-log.md) records representative executed queries and access failures. The [snapshot manifest](literature-snapshot.json) hashes selected local inputs and resolves five external branch heads to commits. It does not archive the entire review or establish that those heads were compiled. Local documents can change during this shared campaign; their hashes identify the input initially read.

## Production and HFT implications

The current contract bounds selected structural cleanup per runtime service hook. It does not bound an event containing arbitrarily many hooks, a resize copy, frame initialization, a foreign call, page faults or backing allocation. No cited work upgrades this into a hardware deadline by analogy. A useful next result is a smaller critical-path allocation/copy obligation, with bounds explicitly preserved and capacity tested under bursts.

Separate active dead roots from queued dead owners and payloads, and both from caches and pool rounding. Borrowing can suppress RC operations while retaining old graphs. Deferred decrements can suppress uniqueness and therefore increase copying. Queue fairness can hold while a small ancestor conceals a large descendant. Capacity failure can occur even when long-run average service exceeds average arrival. These are different mechanisms and need separate diagnostics.

Relevant load families are bursts followed by predeclared recovery windows, sustained overload, large fan-out destruction, a small chain ending in a large payload, active shared descendants, repeated snapshot replacement, mixed owner-frame/temp/object work and zero-allocation paths. Record response time including waiting, capacity failure and incomplete arrivals. A percentile over only completed fast events can conceal overload collapse.

Prior Minyar experiments already disfavor simple novelty narratives: FIFO sometimes retained less and had lower response tails than current scheduling, eager sometimes had better tail response, and the stack admission experiment had no decisive latency win. Keep those outcomes in the research history. A modest proven allocation reduction can be a good production improvement without claiming that all automatic memory is solved.

## Submission gate

Before a paper, recover the remaining closest full texts, check the newest versions, extract theorem assumptions and implementation obligations, reproduce at least the strongest equivalent baseline and publish failed variants. This pass supports prioritized experiments and accurate comparisons; it does not certify exhaustive paper coverage or prove novelty.
