## What this does NOT establish for hard real-time or HFT

No accepted finding bounds total wall latency, an event's number of service hooks, copying/UTF8 traversal, allocator calls, OS page faults, cache/TLB misses, scheduler interference, I/O or real GPU work. Pretouching a fixed pool does not lock its pages. Lazy reservation does not guarantee future physical commitment.

The closed-phase potential is not an open-world arrival/backlog/admission bound. Arrival rate, burst envelope, representation-induced future work, service opportunities, object sizes and lifetimes must be related explicitly. A small pending-task count is not a storage certificate. Idle service cannot bank credit against future mortal-prefix scan debt.

Equal semantic outputs, equal cumulative cleanup units, successful reuse or fewer allocations do not establish equivalent retention or finite-pool admission. Shared descendants, flattened view roots, cached immortals, rounding, fragmentation and simultaneous resize buffers all matter. Single-mutator/non-reentrant ownership is an explicit premise; no global concurrency guarantee follows by analogy to CIRC, distributed Microgrid counting or parallel Work Packets.

Paired workload CPU estimates and bootstrap intervals describe the observed pairs and their independence assumptions. They do not establish statistical generalization to different programs/hardware or extreme-tail guarantees. Paced soaks, sparse samples, sanitizer executions and observer journals have different measurement purposes. Completed fast events alone would also miss dropped/failed arrivals and queueing delay.

These are boundaries of actual evidence and supported semantics. Unsupported closures, finalizers or other absent language features were not invented as deficiencies. Existing finite-heap fatal failure is not silently changed into recoverable OOM. Source/compiler preservation and whole-repository final integration remain separately scoped, with the stopped lane left untouched.

## Two unexecuted research hypotheses

**H1: queue-empty buddy admission refinement for scalar appended Lists.** The final guard leaves an interesting precise question: with matched complete allocator state, identical scalar source/capacity, header-first service, no intervening operation/reentrancy, and the declared final-block precondition, does direct reservation preserve admission for every later raw-allocation continuation? Start with a mathematical statement over exact split/free-list order; no production change is needed. Accept only an induction over arbitrary buddy depth covering moving and in-place growth, or a clearly narrower theorem with explicit preconditions. Reject the broad hypothesis on one complete baseline-success/candidate-failure continuation. This is a potential transformation-specific result, not new preallocation, and publication priority remains open.

**H2: representation-aware closed-cohort cleanup-demand certificate.** A small fixed-arity/length-bounded acyclic subset may permit an offline certificate charging future owner scans/finalizations and retained block classes before they become queued tasks. It must include first-mortal List promotion, hidden descendants, shared-DAG identity, view roots, frame/chunk capacities, caches, rounding and resize overlap. Accept only a sound, useful bound with no runtime graph scan/new syntax or expensive whole-program machinery. Reject any undercharge or assertion that total-byte capacity proves contiguous admission. Collector-sensitive heap analyses, CTRC and network calculus already occupy the general ideas; the possible contribution is this exact representation/accounting relation. This is unproved and unexecuted, with compiler integration outside this lane.

These cards do not authorize or request revival of restricted execution. The already proposed aggregate ASCII certificate remains a plausible **engineering** follow-up: a local propagation rule, if its actual baseline justifies it. It is not relabeled a novel research direction or reported as accepted.
